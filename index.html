Show HN: Every satellite that passed over any place this week, and its photos

2026-11-09 @ 17:51:31Points: 388Comments: 67

type in any place and it finds every satellite that passed over it in the last 7 days (SGP4 on the CelesTrak catalog, tracking 17k+ objects and runs in your browser in about half a second), then pulls the real Sentinel-2 / Landsat / NASA images from those passes.
