It's been a major struggle to get it working consistently, though. It's hard enough to have ONE character be consistent in a series of images you create using image generation models like nano banana, but to maintain consistency of up to 4 main characters, all the side characters, the props (like dragons, submarines, spaceships, or what have you) throughout the book is a Herculean struggle! On top of that, I got really ambitious and wanted to do a worldwide launch supporting 24 languages - you can imagine the amount of testing it takes! It really buried me - especially as a side project.

I nearly gave up on it. After I launched last year, I got a few orders and the customers who got the books (primarily in English in the U.S.) were happy, but I did not feel comfortable publicizing the product, so it languished. But then Fable came along and gave me hope. After a few months working with the great capabilities of Fable and Claude Code, I was able to relaunch - this time in fewer languages and countries.

As the public starts to use it, they are stretching the limits of what's possible, so it's a constant struggle, but it's also a great learning and hardening experience! To others struggling with similar quests, I would strongly recommend the superpowers framework for Claude Code - and building in the testing is the most important thing you could do to succeed. I would also love to hear if you have any advice for me.

The website itself was created using the impeccable plugin and Google fonts - and design and consumer-friendliness was paramount. I am very pleased with it, and customers have said that they love it. There are tons of examples - mostly AI-generated family photos and the comics and picture books created using those photos.

For example, https://joonoo.art/books/mystery-of-the-lighthouse-key/read shows you a photo of 3 siblings and the full comic book that was created using that photo.

You can find tons more example books and comics to scroll through in video form here: https://joonoo.art/videos. If you'd rather scroll through the pages, start here: https://joonoo.art/comics or here: https://joonoo.art/picture-books
