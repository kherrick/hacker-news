So I built siltop: btop's calm, with eyes on the silicon. Real power, clocks and temps straight from the SMC and IOReport, no sudo, about 1.3% of one core (btop is ~2.5% on the same machine).

10-second try, no load needed:

Rust, MIT/Apache, no telemetry, no network unless you turn on update checks.
