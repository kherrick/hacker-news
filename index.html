Show HN: SimDrop – Running iOS simulators in the macOS Finder sidebar

2026-11-26 @ 16:51:38Points: 24Comments: 6

I kept running into two annoyances: getting config files in and out of a simulator is clunky (digging into CoreSimulator UUID folders or running simctl), and on the iPhone Duo simulator I couldn't even drag and drop images onto the window at all.

SimDrop puts every running iOS simulator in Finder's sidebar (under Locations) with two folders:

- Downloads/ (Files app > On My iPhone > Downloads) - Photos and Videos/ (the simulator's Photos library)

Simulators appear when they boot and disappear when they shut down. Everything is also reachable from the terminal under ~/Library/CloudStorage/SimDrop-SimDrop/.

A couple of caveats up front: - Dragging moves files by default (same as iCloud Drive, because it uses File Provider on the startup volume)—hold Option to copy. - macOS 15+ and iOS simulators only (not physical devices).

It's free, open source (GPL-3.0), signed and notarized, and installable via Homebrew: 

  brew install --cask rxliuli/tap/sim-drop
Just sharing in case others run into the same friction—feedback or PRs welcome!
